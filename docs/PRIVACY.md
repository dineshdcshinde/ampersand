# Ampersand privacy policy

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

Ampersand is a browser extension that shows the query parameters of the page you're on and lets you edit them. This policy explains what it does with your data.

## The short version

Ampersand reads the address of the tab you open it on, so it can show and edit that address. That data stays in your browser. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers.

## What Ampersand handles, and why

| Data | When | Why | Where it's kept | How long |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The current tab's URL | Only when you click the Ampersand icon (or press its shortcut) | To show its parameters and update them when you click **Update URL** | In memory while the popup is open | Until the popup closes |
| Previous URLs of that tab | When you click **Update URL** | So **Undo last update** can take you back | Chrome's session storage, on your device | Cleared when you close the browser |
| Parameters you unticked | When you click **Update URL** | So they stay in the list, in the same place, ready to tick back on | Chrome's session storage, on your device | Cleared when you close the browser |
| Your setting ("Don't reload the page") | When you change it | To remember your choice | Chrome's sync storage | Until you remove the extension |

If you're signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome may sync that one setting between your devices. That's done by Google's Chrome Sync, not by Ampersand. No URLs are ever put in sync storage.

## What Ampersand does not do

- It doesn't send any data off your device. It makes no network requests at all.
- It doesn't read page content, form data, passwords or cookies.
- It doesn't run in the background or watch the sites you visit. It only acts when you open it.
- It doesn't sell, share or transfer data to anyone.
- It doesn't use data for advertising, creditworthiness or lending, or anything unrelated to its single purpose.

## Permissions

- **activeTab**: read and update the address of the tab you opened Ampersand on, and only that tab, only when you open it.
- **storage**: keep the setting, undo history and unticked parameters described above, on your device.
- **scripting**: used only if you turn on **Don't reload the page**, to change the address bar of the current tab without reloading it.

## Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

Ampersand's use of information complies with the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies), including the Limited Use requirements.
